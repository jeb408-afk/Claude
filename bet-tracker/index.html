Breakdown

Ledger

Odds are stored and shown in American format; decimal odds (for example 1.91) are converted on entry. Profit counts won, lost and cashed-out bets; pushes and voids return the stake and are left out of staked totals, ROI and win rate. ROI is net profit divided by total staked on those bets.

Expected wins sum the implied probability of each settled win or loss. The standard deviation is the square root of the summed p(1 − p), so a difference inside one standard deviation is consistent with chance. Closing line value (CLV) compares the decimal odds you took with the closing odds you record: positive CLV means you beat the closing price.
