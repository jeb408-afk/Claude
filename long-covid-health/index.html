Load your Apple Health export to see activity, resting heart rate, HRV and heart rate patterns tied to post-exertional malaise (PEM), orthostatic symptoms and recovery over time. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

iPhone: Health app → profile picture → Export All Health Data. Save the zip to Files, then choose it here or drop it on this box. Large exports (1 GB+) take a minute or two.
