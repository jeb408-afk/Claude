# Long COVID Steps & Heart Rate

`long-covid-health/index.html` is a single-file page that reads an Apple Health export (`export.zip` or `export.xml`) in the browser and shows steps and heart rate data relevant to long COVID recovery. Nothing is uploaded.

Open the file in Chrome, Safari or Firefox, then choose your export. On iPhone: Health app → profile picture → Export All Health Data.

What it shows:
- Last-30-day summary: steps, resting HR, HRV, minutes above estimated anaerobic threshold, sleep, strained days
- Daily status calendar from resting HR and HRV vs. a 28-day personal baseline (Stable, Watch, Strained, Crash)
- Steps with a comfort ceiling (energy envelope) and strain rate by step count
- Resting HR change in the days after exertion (PEM pattern)
- Daily HR range, minutes above threshold (baseline RHR + 15 bpm), hours of high HR at low activity (orthostatic / POTS signal), HR and steps by hour of day
- Walking HR, HR recovery, SpO2, respiratory rate, VO2 max, sleep, walking speed, distance, flights, exercise minutes
- Recovery vs. pre-illness levels when you enter a COVID onset date
- Exertion-day outcomes, weekly summary, daily table, CSV copy

Not medical advice.
